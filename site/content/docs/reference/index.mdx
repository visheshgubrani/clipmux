- [API reference](/docs/reference/api): every route of the integration API,
  with its parameters, an example and its errors.
- [Webhook events](/docs/reference/webhooks): the events your endpoints
  receive, their headers, and how to verify them.
- [Errors](/docs/reference/errors): every error code, and what to do.
- [Limits and defaults](/docs/reference/limits): rate limits, sizes and token
  lifetimes.
- [Environment variables](/docs/reference/configuration): every setting of
  the server, the worker, the CLI and your app's backend.
- [Command line](/docs/reference/cli): `clipmux` and `npx @clipmux/node`.
- [Changelog](/docs/reference/changelog): what changed in each release.

The integration API is also described as OpenAPI 3.1, which every ClipMux
instance serves at `/openapi.json` without a credential. This site's copy is
at [/openapi.json](/openapi.json).
