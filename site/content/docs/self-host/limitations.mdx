ClipMux is deliberately small: one Go binary and Postgres. This page lists
what it doesn't do today, plainly, so nothing here surprises you in
production.

## Playback and protection

- **No DRM.** Videos are encrypted with AES-128 and keys are handed only to
  valid tokens, but anyone with a valid token can fetch the key, and common
  download tools do. It's deterrence, not Widevine or FairPlay. See
  [What encryption does and doesn't do](/docs/build/concepts/encryption).
- **Playback shares the dashboard's origin by default.** Until you set a
  media URL on another domain, videos play from the same origin as the
  dashboard. See [Built-in delivery](/docs/self-host/delivery/built-in).

## Scale

- **One server.** One serve replica is the supported shape. Rate limits,
  the route cache, the speech and chapters runners and the shutdown drain
  live in one process. There's no high-availability setup.
- **No audit log.** Owner actions and key operations are logged with their
  request IDs, but nothing durable records who did what.
- **No OpenTelemetry tracing.** There are Prometheus metrics and structured
  logs. See [Monitoring](/docs/self-host/operate/monitoring).

## Subtitles and chapters

- **One caption language per video.** whisper.cpp detects the language, and
  a video gets one subtitles track. There's no translation and no language
  hint.
- **No uploaded captions.** Subtitles are generated. You can't upload your
  own caption file, or edit a transcript.
- **No spend cap on speech APIs.** Every transcription sent to a speech API
  is billed by the provider. ClipMux neither estimates nor caps it.
- **No spend estimate for chapters.** Each video's chapters are billed by
  the LLM provider, and ClipMux doesn't estimate the cost.

## Encoding

- **Provider spend is an estimate.** For Modal, it's Modal's bill up to its
  last reported hour plus an estimate since; for RunPod, an estimate from
  the hourly rate set in Connect. A machine that runs past its reservation
  isn't stopped at the limit, so a month can overshoot by that much.
- **The PC agent is command-line only.** The dashboard doesn't list PCs,
  create pairing codes or revoke them. A PC is paired with
  `clipmux worker --server` and approved in the dashboard, or with a code
  from `clipmux admin create-pairing`; the rest is the API.

## Webhooks and outside services

- **Owner-set URLs aren't filtered for private addresses.** That's by
  design for webhook endpoints, the speech API and the chapters LLM, so
  servers on your own network work. Only the owner can set them.

## Cloudflare

- **Edge analytics are sampled.** Cloudflare's Analytics Engine samples data
  points, so edge views, watch time and bytes are close estimates, and edge
  unique viewers can read low. See
  [Edge analytics](/docs/self-host/delivery/edge-analytics).
- **Disconnect leaves the Worker behind.** Disconnecting Cloudflare stops
  the Worker serving your videos, but the Worker, its custom domain and the
  token stay in your Cloudflare account, since ClipMux keeps no token to
  remove them. Delete them in Cloudflare.

## Packages

- **ESM only.** `@clipmux/node`, `@clipmux/player` and `@clipmux/uploader`
  are ES modules. A CommonJS app needs Node 20.19 or later (or 22.12) to
  `require()` them, and TypeScript's `moduleResolution: node` can't resolve
  their subpath exports.
